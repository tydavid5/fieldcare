              Danger signs to know by heart
              • Difficulty breathing or fast breathing
              • Chest pain
              • Convulsions or fits
              • Unconscious, or very drowsy and hard to wake
              • Bleeding that will not stop
              • No urine, very dry mouth, sunken eyes, or no tears when crying
              • High fever with a stiff neck or a rash
              • Swollen face, hands or feet with headache or blurred vision in pregnancy
              • A baby who will not feed

              Any one of these means urgent referral. Do not wait for the care plan.

              How it works

              1. On-device, offline. The triage check runs on the device with Gemini Nano, so it works with no signal and patient details stay on the device.
              2. Cloud, when online. When the device finds signal, Gemini Flash in the cloud adds a fuller care plan, a short list of possible causes, and what to watch for.
              3. Offline queue. Cases assessed offline are saved on the device and sync their care plan when the connection returns.
